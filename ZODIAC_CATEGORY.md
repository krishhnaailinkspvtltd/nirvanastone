# Shree Nirvan — Zodiac Bracelets

Twelve product photo references sourced from the owner-supplied `all_images.zip` and mapping file.

Each card maps to one zodiac sign, traditional qualities and stones visible in the reference.
Images were cropped to remove external store promotional headings and gift stickers; original source product remains the reference.

**Review before selling:** image usage rights; photos may show a third-party product; gemstones, charm material, bead sizes, price, stock and free gifts are not confirmed. Prices shown are source-store reference prices only.

Benefits shown on the page are **traditional interpretations**, symbolic intentions and cultural belief, not health, financial, relationship or scientific promises. Dates are common Western sun-sign calendar; Vedic rashis may differ.
